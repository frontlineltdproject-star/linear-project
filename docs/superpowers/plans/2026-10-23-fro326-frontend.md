## Review focus

1. Moving an action between tabs must not erase its draft, duplicate an email send, or leave a stale confirmation attached to a different Order.
2. View-only users must keep authorized read access, while mutation controls and unavailable records remain guarded exactly as before.
3. Terminal collis and inherited files must remain inspectable with their original identity/provenance; layout must not make them selectable or editable.
4. Narrow Greek layouts must not hide action explanations or force the expanded form to inherit the table's desktop width.
5. Refetches and failures must not silently reset unsaved inputs or present incomplete data as an empty successful result.
