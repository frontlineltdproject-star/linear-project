# Share ΑΛΚ through verified registration coverage

Accepted on 21 October 2026 during FRO-356 review. Splitting goods does not create a new customs registration, so verified covered Orders share one current ΑΛΚ reference, including when it is entered or corrected after the split; independent copied values would drift. Updates must be atomic and audited across that coverage without rewriting issued documents or historical facts, while duplication for new goods starts without registration coverage or ΑΛΚ.
